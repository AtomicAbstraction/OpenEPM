# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Open EPM community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy toward others.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `contactbhasker7483@gmail.com`. The project owner may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
