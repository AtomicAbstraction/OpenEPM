# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public Issues, Discussions, or pull requests.

Email `contactbhasker7483@gmail.com` with:

- the affected component and version or commit;
- reproduction steps and required preconditions;
- the observed and expected behavior;
- the potential impact; and
- any suggested mitigation.

Do not include credentials, personal data, or data from systems you are not authorized to test. Receipt will be acknowledged when practical. A remediation or disclosure timeline will be determined after validation and impact assessment.

No public release is currently declared supported. Supported versions will be listed here when the first qualified release is published.
